Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How do I book a stay for my dachshund?

Call us at (404) 647-4904, or email apavluck@gmail.com. We'll get back to you within 24 hours to discuss your dachshund's specific needs and find the perfect spot in our schedule.

What should I bring for my dachshund's stay?

Just their regular food, any necessary medications, a beloved toy or blanket, and any special instructions for their routine. If they're a swimmer, we even have a pool.

Do you really only take one dog at a time?

Always. It's the heart of what we do. Your dachshund will never share their day with another dog, which keeps things calm, safe, and completely focused on them.
